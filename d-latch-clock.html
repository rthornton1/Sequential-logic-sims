Clocked D Latch Simulator

Here a clock drives the latch's enable input. While CLK = 1, Q follows D. While CLK = 0, Q holds its value.

Timing diagram

Shaded bands show when CLK = 1. Inside a band Q follows D; outside it Q holds.

Things to try

1. While CLK = 1, click D a few times. Q follows every change.
2. While CLK = 0, click D. Q doesn't change. This is the 1-bit memory. When CLK goes back to 1, Q takes the new value of D.
3. Click "Pause clock" to freeze the clock and the timing diagram. Then use the CLK button to step the clock by hand.
